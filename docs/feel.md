# 🥋 Feel brief — what Karate Kids should feel like

This is the bar. A change isn't done when the tests pass; it's done when a bout
on a phone makes you want another one.

## The one-line version
**Two fighters circle, one strike lands, and the whole screen admits it.**

## The moment that matters: contact
Karate Kids has no health bars. Every exchange builds to one clean hit. That hit
has to land like a board cracking.

- **Time stops.** On contact the game freezes for a few frames — about 90ms for
  an ippon, 60ms for a waza-ari. The frozen frame is the full-extension frame,
  so the player *sees* the strike connect.
- **It flashes.** The fighter who got hit blinks white for a beat, and a burst
  of sparks (punches) or dust (sweeps) goes off at the exact point of contact.
- **The camera flinches.** A short, sharp shake, and a quick punch-in toward the
  strike. An ippon hits harder than a waza-ari.
- **The phone kicks back.** A short vibration on contact, longer for an ippon.
- **It sounds like a hit.** A thump with a crack on top, not a beep.

## The call is a moment, not a caption
The referee's call is the payoff. **IPPON** slams in big, overshoots and
settles. The score digit pops. On the winning point everything drops into
slow motion for a beat, then the winner's name lands.

## Between the hits: tension
- Every technique **whooshes** as it starts, so a whiff still feels committed.
- Kicks leave a brief **afterimage** trail through the air.
- The dojo is warm and lit, with the edges falling into shadow so the fighters
  own the frame.

## Rules for the juice
- **Presentation only.** Nothing here may change the simulation. Hit-stop pauses
  the clock, not the rules; the same seed still produces the same checksum.
- **Respect reduced motion.** With it on: no shake, no flash, no slow motion.
  The information stays; only the violence goes.
- **60fps on a mid-range phone.** Particles are pooled, and nothing allocates
  per frame.
- **Readable first.** The effect must never hide the fighters, the score, or the
  sticks.

## Done means
A bout played on a real phone where every landed hit makes you flinch a
little, every ippon feels earned, and the first thing you do after the
winner's name appears is start another one.
